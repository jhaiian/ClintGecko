Clint is an open source Android browser with a modern Material 3 interface and tons of features. It has no backend, collects zero data, and has no ads or paywalls. Completely free.

<b>MEDIA CAPTURE</b>
• Detects video, audio and subtitle streams on the pages you visit
• Supports normal video, HLS/M3U8 and DASH streams
• Download detected media directly from the browser

<b>DOWNLOAD MANAGER</b>
• Multi-threaded and multi-part downloads
• Pause and resume
• Download scheduler and speed limiter
• Custom download locations
• Automatically pauses and resumes stuck downloads

<b>QUIVER GUARD</b>
• Built-in ad and tracker blocker
• Cosmetic and network filtering
• Multiple filter lists
• Per-site controls

<b>WEBSITE BLOCKER</b>
• Blocks pornography, gambling and other unwanted categories
• Category-based filter lists
• Add your own custom websites to block

<b>DATA SAVER</b>
• Only on metered networks
• Disable images, block video and stop autoplay
• Block media preloading, web fonts and third-party frames
• Reuse saved pages and request lighter pages
• Disable online search suggestions and favicons
• Block scripts and CSS

<b>PRIVACY AND SECURITY</b>
• Incognito mode
• Popup protection
• Send Do Not Track request
• Passkeys support
• Autofill can be turned off

<b>CUSTOMIZATION</b>
• 50 accent colors, Light and Dark themes and 5 surface intensities
• 500 possible theme combinations
• Choose between the Clint homepage and your search engine homepage
• Plain color, gradient or custom image homepage backgrounds

<b>PROFILES</b>
• Stay signed in to several accounts on the same website at once
• Each profile keeps its own cookies and site data
• Open new tabs and shortcuts in any profile

<b>BROWSER</b>
• Multi-tab browsing
• Reader Mode
• Desktop Mode
• Delete inactive tabs automatically
• Quick Download
• Shortcut Manager with frameless shortcuts

Requires Android 8.0 or higher and a WebView implementation.
