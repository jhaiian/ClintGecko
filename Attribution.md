## Acknowledgments

### Download System — Inspired by 1DM
The download management system in Clint Gecko drew inspiration from the design and user experience of [1DM (1 Download Manager)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=idm.internet.download.manager) by Innobyte. Special credit to the 1DM team for their excellent download management approach, which influenced Clint's download features.

### Theme — Inspired by ytdlnis
The theming system in Clint Gecko drew inspiration from [ytdlnis](https://github.com/deniscerri/ytdlnis) by Denis Cerri. Special credit to the ytdlnis project for its theme design and approach, which influenced Clint's look and feel.
